# Beyond PMBJP — Affordable Products Worth Adding

Companion to `PMBJP-Skincare-Protocol.md` and `PMBJP-Full-Skin-Hair-Catalogue.md`.
Profile: 20 y/o male · 5-year active acne · post-acne hyperpigmentation · dehydrated-yet-oily barrier · daily bus commute.

**On prices:** Indian D2C and pharmacy skincare prices swing constantly across Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa and brand sites — discounts of 20–40% are routine and MRP is rarely what you pay. Every figure below is an **approximate street price**, not a quote. Check two sellers before buying.

**On sources:** much of the "best sunscreen 2026" content online is published by the brands selling the sunscreen. I've leaned on independent formulation review where available and flagged where a claim comes from a brand's own blog. Content was rephrased for compliance with licensing restrictions.

## The honest headline: you need 3 additions, not 12

You are about to start adapalene and azelaic acid on a barrier that is already compromised. The failure mode for someone in your position is not buying too little — it's buying eight new actives, irritating your face in week three, and quitting the whole routine.

So this list is deliberately ranked, and the ranking matters more than the products:

| Priority | What | Why | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sunscreen** | Mandatory. PMBJP has none. Without it the marks won't fade. | ₹250–450 |
| **2** | **Better moisturiser** | Fixes the root cause — the tight-then-oily cycle. | ₹300–450 |
| **3** | **Niacinamide serum 5%** | Oil control + pigment, low irritation risk. | ₹300–600 |
| 4 | Body/back acne wash | Only if you have chest/back/shoulder acne. | ₹160–350 |
| 5 | Everything else | Optional. Genuinely skip until months 3+. | — |

**Buy #1 now. Add #2 next month. Add #3 only once adapalene is at nightly with no irritation.** One new product at a time, two weeks apart, so you know what caused a reaction.

## 1. Sunscreen — the mandatory purchase

Non-negotiable for you. Target spec: **SPF 30–50, PA+++ or PA++++, gel/fluid/emulgel texture, oil-free, non-comedogenic.** PA rating matters as much as SPF here, because UVA is what drives pigmentation and what passes through bus windows.

### My pick for you
**Deconstruct Gel SPF 50 PA++++** *or* **La Shield Pollution Protect SPF 50** — whichever is cheaper the week you buy. Both are oil-free gels with strong UVA cover, which is exactly the spec your marks need. If your bumps turn out to be fungal, choose La Shield.

### The two things that actually determine whether it works
1. **Quantity.** Two finger-lengths for face and neck, roughly ¼ teaspoon for the face alone. Most people apply a third of what's needed and get a third of the labelled SPF. A 50 g tube used properly lasts about **5–6 weeks**. If yours lasts three months, you're underapplying.
2. **Reapplication.** For your commute, buy the cheapest adequate sunscreen and use enough of it, rather than an expensive one you ration. If you're out past midday, a second application beats a higher SPF. A small decanted bottle or a sunscreen stick in your bag solves this.

## 2. Moisturiser — the upgrade that fixes the root cause

PMBJP code 1960 (aloe + vitamin E) is adequate. But your specific complaint — tight after washing, greasy by noon — is **barrier lipid loss**, and the fix is ceramides, which PMBJP does not stock at all.

## 3. Niacinamide serum — target 5%, not 10%

Niacinamide does four things you need at once: reduces sebum, suppresses melanin transfer (so it fades marks), supports the barrier, and is anti-inflammatory. PMBJP has no niacinamide serum.

**Go with 5%, not 10%.** This is the opposite of what most marketing pushes, and it's the right call for you. Even Deconstruct — who sell both — [state that 2–5% is effective for oil control and tone while staying gentle enough for daily use, and that 10%+ adds little benefit while raising irritation risk](https://thedeconstruct.in/blogs/skin-information/best-niacinamide-serum-for-oily-skin-india). You will already be running adapalene *and* azelaic acid on a dehydrated barrier. Do not add a 10% serum to that.

| Product | Notes | Approx. price |
|---|---|---|
| **Deconstruct 5% Niacinamide + 2% Alpha Arbutin Clearing Serum** | The best-matched option here: 5% niacinamide plus alpha arbutin, [targeted specifically at post-acne marks and uneven tone](https://thedeconstruct.in/products/clearing-serum) — and alpha arbutin is a tyrosinase inhibitor that PMBJP has no equivalent for. | ~₹400–600 |
| **Dr Sheth's / The Derma Co / Mamaearth 5% niacinamide** | Cheaper, widely discounted, perfectly serviceable. | ~₹250–400 |
| **Minimalist 10% Niacinamide + Zinc + Matmarine** | Rated by DermApproved as [among the strongest niacinamide serums at any price, around ₹599, with published INCI and pH](https://dermapproved.com/products/minimalist-10-niacinamide-face-serum/). Excellent product — but the 10% strength is more than you need right now. Consider it at month 4+. | ~₹500–600 |

**When to add it:** only after adapalene is at nightly with no irritation — realistically week 10+. Use it in the **morning**, under moisturiser and sunscreen. It's the one active that's fine in daylight.

## What NOT to buy — where your money gets wasted

This section is worth more than the recommendations.

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| **Face scrubs / apricot or walnut scrubs** | Physical scrubs on active acne rupture lesions and cause more inflammation, which means **more dark marks**. You already have chemical exfoliation from salicylic acid, adapalene and azelaic acid. Never scrub. |
| **Alcohol-based toners, "oil control" astringents** | Strip the barrier, worsen the exact tight-then-oily cycle you're trying to break. |
| **"Fairness" creams and skin-lightening creams** | Frequently contain undisclosed steroids, mercury or hydroquinone. This is the same trap as PMBJP codes 459/2563/2564. |
| **Daily clay masks / charcoal masks** | Once a week at most. Daily use is straightforward barrier damage. |
| **Anything labelled "instant results" or "7-day fairness"** | Post-acne marks take 3–6 months. Anything faster is either a steroid or a lie. |
| **Neem/multani mitti/lemon/toothpaste/besan home remedies** | Lemon juice is a photosensitiser at low pH — actively dangerous on a sunny commute. Toothpaste burns skin. |
| **10-step routines, expensive essences, snail mucin, exosomes** | You need five products. Skin does not improve with product count. |
| **Face wipes** | Smear sebum around, contain irritating preservatives. |
| **Rs 2,000+ "acne kits" from clinics or Instagram** | The entire evidence-based routine costs under ₹1,200 including PMBJP items. |
| **Vitamin C + retinoid on the same night, or too many actives at once** | Guaranteed irritation. The most common reason routines fail. |
| **Biotin supplements for acne** | No evidence for acne, and it skews thyroid and troponin lab tests. |

## The final combined routine

Codes = PMBJP. Everything else is from this document.

### Morning
1. Cleanse — **PMBJP 2259**, lukewarm water, 20–30 seconds
2. Diluted glycerin — **PMBJP 715**, 1:9 with water, on damp skin
3. *(month 3+)* **5% niacinamide serum**
4. Moisturiser — **ceramide cream**, or PMBJP 1960
5. **Sunscreen** — two finger-lengths, face + neck + ears. Never skip.

### Midday, on the bus
Blot with tissue. Reapply sunscreen if you can. Don't wash your face.

### Evening
1. Cleanse — **PMBJP 2259** (removes sunscreen, sebum, pollution particulates)
2. Dry fully, 5–10 minutes
3. **Alternate nights:** **PMBJP 110** adapalene (pea-sized, whole face) / **PMBJP 2391** azelaic acid
4. Moisturiser — **ceramide cream**
5. *(as needed)* hydrocolloid patch on any single inflamed spot

### Weekly
Body: **salicylic body wash** in the shower, 60 seconds contact.
*Only if you suspect fungal acne:* **PMBJP 2365** ketoconazole shampoo as a 5-minute face/chest mask, twice weekly.

## Three things that cost nothing and matter more than half this list

1. **Change your pillowcase twice a week.** Sebum, sweat and product residue accumulate against your face for 8 hours a night.
2. **Stop touching and picking.** Every squeezed lesion becomes a mark that takes 3–6 months to fade. This single habit change will do more for your pigmentation than any serum here.
3. **Wipe your phone screen.** It sits against your jaw and cheek daily.

And the honest closing point, unchanged from the earlier documents: **5 years of continuous active acne with marks is the standard threshold for oral therapy.** No optimisation of topicals substitutes for that assessment. PMBJP sells isotretinoin at ₹28–47 per strip (codes 2111, 1670) — the drug is affordable; the consult is what you're missing.
